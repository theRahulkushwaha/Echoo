What you need to do every time you switch networks
Step 1 — Find new IP:
cmdipconfig
Look for Wi-Fi → IPv4 Address — e.g. 192.168.2.10
Step 2 — Update .env:
envEXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL=http://192.168.2.10:3000
Step 3 — Start Expo with new IP:
bashnpx expo start --clear --host 192.168.2.10
